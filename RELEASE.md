Connect an iPhone or Mac companion to a nearby Switch through a modified Switch.

- **NRO:** copy it to `/switch/ldn-relay/` on the modified Switch's SD card.
- **Switch ZIP:** the same NRO with installation instructions, licenses and build information.
- **SHA256SUMS:** checksums for the downloads.
- **build-info.json:** exact source commit and pinned build toolchain.

Launch through **Album / Applet Mode**, open your companion, and press **A** to
connect. Press **A** again at the prompt to approve it. No pairing key is needed.

For Pokémon, use the separate [mGBA LDN iOS/Mac companion](https://github.com/veritr1x/mgba-ldn-ios-macos).
This download is the relay; it does not include games or an emulator.

This is a preview. Reported trades work, but broader device and recovery testing
is incomplete. See [installation](https://github.com/veritr1x/ldn-relay/blob/main/INSTALL.md)
and [known limits](https://github.com/veritr1x/ldn-relay/blob/main/PRODUCTION_READINESS.md).
